# Contributing to RKSkillTube (R.K. SkillForge)

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **RKSkillTube**! 🚀

RKSkillTube is a modern, scalable video learning platform built to bridge the gap in educational resources and guidance—particularly for Tier-3 college students and self-taught learners. The platform supports free and paid courses, creator channels, video hosting and transcoding, YouTube-curated course playlists, community memberships, AI-driven learning recommendations, and an administrative moderation ecosystem.

This guide outlines the development standards, architectural foundations, workflow, and expectations for all contributors.
